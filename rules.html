Important Traffic Rules

  1. Wear a properly fitted and fastened helmet on a two-wheeler.
  2. Wear a seat belt while travelling in a car.
  3. Follow traffic lights and posted speed limits.
  4. Never use a mobile phone while driving.
  5. Never drive under the influence of alcohol.
  6. Give way to emergency vehicles.
  7. Respect pedestrian crossings.

Helmet and Seat-Belt Safety

Helmet Safety

Helmets help protect riders against serious head injuries.

Seat-Belt Safety

Seat belts help reduce injuries in sudden stops and crashes.

Common Causes of Accidents

  • Overspeeding
  • Distracted driving
  • Ignoring traffic signals
  • Driving while tired
